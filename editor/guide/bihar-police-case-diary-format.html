Bihar Police case diary format and how to write one

BP Diary is a free browser tool that helps you write Bihar Police case diaries (also called kand dainiki or केस डायरी) and official letters in Hindi. This guide explains what goes into a case diary, how the format works, and how to use BP Diary to write one.

Note: This is not an official Bihar Police resource. BP Diary is an independent tool built to help investigating officers. For official work, use the CCTNS portal.

What is a case diary?

A case diary is a daily record kept by the police officer investigating the case (BNSS Section 192). The case diary documents the daily progress of the investigation, actions taken, statements recorded, and evidence collected.

The diary is not meant to be disclosed to the accused or their lawyer during the trial, but the court may refer to it to verify the investigating officer's testimony.

Case diary format

BP Diary follows the layout of Schedule 47, Form 120A (अनुसूची 47, प्रपत्र सं० 120 अ), which is the standard format for Bihar Police case diaries. Each entry typically includes:

  • Date and time: When the investigation action was taken
  • Place: Where the action occurred (police station, crime scene, witness residence, etc.)
  • Action taken: What steps were performed (visited the scene, questioned a witness, seized evidence, sent items to the forensic lab, etc.)
  • Statements: Summaries of statements recorded from witnesses, complainants, or other parties
  • Seizures: Details of any items or evidence seized, with descriptions and seizure memos
  • Next steps: What further investigation is planned

Entries are written in plain language and should be factual, clear, and chronological.

How to write a case diary in Hindi with BP Diary

BP Diary provides three ways to type in Hindi:

1. Hinglish typing (transliteration)

Toggle on the A:अ button in the header. Now type in English letters (for example, "gavah") and you will see Hindi suggestions (for example, "गवाह"). Use the spacebar or arrow keys to select the correct word. This is the fastest way to type Hindi if you are comfortable with English typing.

2. Voice typing

On desktop, press Ctrl + Shift + D or click the microphone icon in the header. Speak in Hindi and your speech will be converted to text. This feature also works on mobile if your browser supports it. Voice typing is useful if you want to dictate a diary entry while away from the keyboard.

3. Punctuation panel

To insert Hindi punctuation marks (full stop, comma, question mark, etc.), open the punctuation panel from the format toolbar and click on the mark you need. It will be inserted at the cursor position.

A4 print and PDF export

Documents in BP Diary are formatted for A4 paper with the standard case diary header. To print or save as PDF:

  • Press Ctrl + P or click the print icon in the header
  • In the browser print dialog, select "Save as PDF"
  • Save the file to your computer or mobile device

The printed output will match the Bihar Police form layout, complete with the header showing "अनुसूची 47, प्रपत्र सं० 120 अ".

Where is your data stored?

All files you write in BP Diary are stored locally in your browser (using IndexedDB). BP Diary does not send your data to any server. If you want to back up your files or access them from another device, you can enable optional Google Drive backup from the History panel. The backup goes to your personal Google Drive account, not to BP Diary servers (we do not have any servers).

Frequently asked questions

Is BP Diary free?
Yes, BP Diary is completely free to use with no hidden charges.
Can I use BP Diary on mobile?
Yes, BP Diary works in any modern browser on mobile, tablet, or computer.
Do I need internet to use BP Diary?
Hinglish typing requires an internet connection because it uses Google Input Tools. Voice typing and Drive backup also need internet. If you type in Hindi using a Hindi keyboard, you can work without these features.
Can I open my files on another device?
Only if you have enabled Google Drive backup. Without backup, files are stored locally and will not sync to other devices.
Is my data safe?
Yes, your data stays on your device. BP Diary does not collect, store, or send your data to any external server. Drive backup is optional and goes to your own Google Drive, which you control.
Is this an official Bihar Police tool?
No, BP Diary is an independent tool built to help investigating officers. It is not affiliated with Bihar Police or any government department. For official services, visit the CCTNS citizen portal.
Can I use BP Diary for other types of documents?
Yes, BP Diary also has a Letter template for official letters. You can switch between Diary and Letter modes from the header.
